Laundry Desk — How to Use

Mobile-first guide for Admin, Manager, Delivery and Customer users.

1. Sign in

Use your staff login ID or customer UAE mobile number with the six-digit code. The app opens the workspace allowed for that account.

2. Quick workflow — default

Pickup Requested → Collected → Received → In Progress → Ready → Delivered

Quick Flow is the normal operating mode. It minimizes taps. Internally Laundry Desk still records the detailed workflow for audit and reporting.

  • Wash + Iron: In Progress can include Washing and Ironing.
  • Iron Only: Washing is skipped automatically.
  • Pickup delivery: when Ready, use Assign + send delivery to assign a driver and dispatch in one step.
  • Counter order: Ready can go directly to Delivered.

3. Detailed stages when required

Open an order and choose Detailed stages. The full stages are Pickup Requested, Collected, Received, Washing, Ironing, Ready, Out for Delivery and Delivered. Switch back to Quick flow at any time.

4. Manager / Admin daily work

  1. Create a Counter Order or Pickup Request.
  2. Assign pickup/delivery drivers when required.
  3. Open an order and use the primary next action.
  4. Use Payment, Driver, Print and WhatsApp from the compact action dock.
  5. Payment status stays separate from laundry status.

5. Reports

Open Reports. Use preset periods: Today, Yesterday, This Week, Last Week, This Month, Last Month, This Year or Custom. You can group results by Day, Week, Month or Year.

Admin / Manager

  • Overview: Orders, pieces, sales, cash received, collected, delivered, average order value, current outstanding and customer credit.
  • Customers: Sales, received, outstanding, advance/credit and net position customer-wise.
  • Operations: Quick workflow queue, detailed stage counts and aging alerts.
  • Payments: Cash received by method and collection point, outstanding, credit added/applied.
  • Calendar: Daily Collected, Delivered and Cash Received values.

Delivery

Reports show only the delivery user's own assigned/completed activity and payments collected by that user.

Customer

Reports show only that customer's own orders, spend, payments, amount due, available credit and calendar activity.

6. Important reporting rule

Sales and Cash Received are different. Example: if an AED 80 order receives AED 100, Sales = AED 80, Cash Received = AED 100 and Customer Credit = AED 20.

Date filters apply to period activity. Current Outstanding, Customer Credit, Ready and In Progress show the current position so balances are not misleading.

7. Admin setup

Admin can manage Organization Name and Address, staff, customer access, garments, services, rates and discounts. Organization details appear in the app header, receipts and print labels.

Laundry Desk — How to Use

Compact guide for Admin, Manager, Delivery and Customer users.

Receive payment

Admin/Manager can tap Receive payment directly from Home or Account against a customer. The same popup is used in both places.

  1. Search/select the customer by name, mobile, Building/Block, Door No. or customer code.
  2. Review Outstanding, Advance/Credit and Net Position. Due amounts use a soft red indicator.
  3. Enter the received amount. The projected result updates live as Remaining due or Advance credit.
  4. Optional: select one or more outstanding orders under Apply to orders. Those orders get priority; any remaining amount continues to the customer's oldest dues. Only the true excess becomes advance.
  5. Select Cash, Card, Bank or Other and save.
Card / Bank: Transaction Ref ID is required. Use Payment details for POS terminal, bank/transfer note or other reconciliation information.

Customer account

The Account popup is not locked to the customer row that opened it. Use the customer search at the top to switch to any customer and immediately load that customer's outstanding orders, advance and finance ledger.

Quick workflow

Requested · Collected · Received · In Progress · Ready · Delivered are flexible forward milestones. Confirmation is off by default and can be enabled by Admin under Setup → Workflow. Garments/services must be captured before moving an order forward.

Garment collection

Choose a service first (Wash Only, Wash + Iron, Iron Only, Dry Clean), then tap garment quantities. Switch services without losing previous lines. A sticky summary keeps garments, services and total price visible.

Pickup routing

Orders can be filtered/sorted by Building/Block. Select several pickup requests, choose the collection boy using chips and assign them in one action. Customers store Building/Block, Door No. and Area/Street separately.

Keyboard & compact UX

Customer search supports ↑/↓, Enter and Esc. Enter submits most forms; missing required fields are focused/highlighted. Orders support multi-status filters; All is exclusive.

Reports

Reports support Excel and PDF download for the selected report/range. Sales and Cash Received remain separate so customer advance/overpayment is not treated as sales.

Admin demo reset

Setup → System → Reset demo data clears transactional/demo data while retaining users, customers and configuration for another test cycle.

Receive Payment: The Home quick action opens a compact account popup. The title/close area and Receive Payment button stay fixed while account history scrolls. Payment method chips are visible in the first view.

Compact Home

Home starts directly with Work by stage, followed by compact Today KPIs, Quick actions and Needs attention. Tap a stage to focus the Home list on that stage; tap All active to return to priority cases. Quick actions remain Counter order, Pickup request, Receive payment and Add customer.

Responsive Home

The Home dashboard now adapts for comfortable reading instead of simply shrinking cards. On phones, Work by Stage is a horizontal swipe strip with larger tap targets, while KPIs and Quick Actions use a readable 2 × 2 layout. Tablet and desktop retain six stage cards plus four KPI and Quick Action cards across the available width.
